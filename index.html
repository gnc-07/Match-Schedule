How to subscribe in your app
  1. Proton Calendar: Settings, Calendars, Other calendars, Add calendar from URL. Paste the link.
  2. Google Calendar (web): Other calendars, the plus sign, From URL. Paste the link.
  3. Apple Calendar: tap Subscribe on an iPhone or Mac, or File, New Calendar Subscription.
  4. Outlook: Add calendar, Subscribe from web. Paste the link.

How it works

  • League fixtures come from openfootball (or football-data.org when configured) and are rebuilt four times a day.
  • When that feed lags, corrections are added from official league and club sites and cross-checked against the press. Friendlies are entered the same way, with every source kept.
  • Live scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard, refreshed every 30 seconds while a match is on. They appear on this site only, not in the calendar.
  • Where CazéTV streams a match free on YouTube, a Watch on CazéTV button appears once the stream is scheduled. Before that, matches on its schedule (taken from the fan-made agendacazetv.com) link to its YouTube channel. Most of its streams only play in Brazil.
  • Line-ups, match events and league tables also come from ESPN. Stadium maps use OpenStreetMap and Wikidata.
  • Stars and settings are saved in your browser only. Finished matches stay on the list, with the final score, for a day after kick-off.
