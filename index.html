How to subscribe in your app
  1. Proton Calendar: Settings, Calendars, Other calendars, Add calendar from URL. Paste the link.
  2. Google Calendar (web): Other calendars, the plus sign, From URL. Paste the link.
  3. Apple Calendar: tap Subscribe on an iPhone or Mac, or File, New Calendar Subscription.
  4. Outlook: Add calendar, Subscribe from web. Paste the link.

How it works

  • League fixtures come from football-data.org (or openfootball if it is unavailable) and are rebuilt four times a day. When that feed lags, and for friendlies, times come from official league and club sites and the press, with every source listed under the match.
  • Live scores, line-ups, match events and league tables come from ESPN; stadium maps from OpenStreetMap and Wikidata. Live scores appear on this site only, not in the calendar.
  • CazéTV buttons lead to its free YouTube streams, found through its channel and the fan-made agendacazetv.com. Most only play in Brazil.
  • Formula 1 session times come from Jolpica-F1 and are marked verified when OpenF1 gives the same time. F1 is not in the calendar.
  • Stars and settings are saved in your browser only. Finished matches stay on the list, with the final score, for a day after kick-off.
