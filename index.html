Reading the list

verified An official source (federation, league, club or official ticketing) gives the time, or at least two independent sources agree.

conflicting reports 1 unconfirmed report Not yet verified. The reported times and every source are listed under the match; hover a source for any note on it.

early Kicks off before 7:00 a.m. in the time zone you selected.

How times are verified

  • League fixtures come from openfootball (or football-data.org when configured) and are rebuilt several times a day.
  • When that feed lags, corrections are added from official league and club sites and cross-checked against the press.
  • Friendlies are entered from federation announcements and press reports, with every source kept.
  • Live scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard and refresh every 30 seconds while a match is on. They appear only on this website, not in the calendar feed.
  • Stars and filters are saved in your browser only. Finished matches drop off two hours after kick-off (or when the live feed says they have ended).
